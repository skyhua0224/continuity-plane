# Continuity Plane Brand Use

[中文](BRANDING.md)

Continuity Plane is the product name. The Apache-2.0 license covers the
software and documentation; it does not grant trademark rights beyond
reasonable use that describes the origin of the work.

## Attribution

Public attribution is optional. Using Continuity Plane does not require an
application UI label, README badge, telemetry event, or public registry entry.
Legal redistribution requirements are defined only by `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.

Projects may use the provided badge:

```markdown
[![Managed with Continuity Plane](docs/assets/managed-with-continuity-plane.svg)](https://github.com/skyhua0224/continuity-plane)
```

The badge means that Continuity Plane helps manage project continuity. It does
not imply endorsement, certification, runtime dependency, or ownership.

## Acceptable Use

- Refer to an unmodified distribution as Continuity Plane.
- Say that a project is managed with or integrates with Continuity Plane.
- Use the supplied badge without changing its wording or implying endorsement.
- Name independent adapters in a way that clearly identifies their publisher.

Modified distributions must not imply that they are official releases. Use a
distinct product name and retain the notices required by Apache-2.0.
